Educational Background

A summary of the key stages of my academic journey, from school through to university and beyond.

01
🏫

Secondary School

During secondary school I was interested in most subjects. I never felt limited by my options and I didn't dwell often on what I wanted to do for a career. I enjoyed creative writing, learning about the Russian Revolution and the Cold War in History, and most of Science and Maths. I struggled in biology however and fainted during a lesson about blood! By the end of my GCSEs I had gravitated towards Physics and had a clear interest in the subject.

02
📚

Sixth Form

I did Maths, Chemistry and Physics in Sixth Form as well as an AS-level in Further Maths. I excelled in theory-based learning but the practical side (especially in Chemistry) came less naturally to me. I overcame this obsatcle with hard work and finished with perfect results.

03
🎓

University

I studied a Master's degree in Physics with Astrophysics at Newcastle University. My learning picked up where I left off in A-Level Physics. I gained experience with Python and Linux during my computational astrophysics modules. My Master's project was a particular highlight. I worked with Dr Chris Harrison, Reader in Astrophysics, to study the relationship between black holes and their host galaxies. My work was credited in the following paper href=https://uhra.herts.ac.uk/id/eprint/26616.
